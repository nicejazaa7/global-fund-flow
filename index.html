How to read this

When someone buys a stock, their cash goes to the seller, so the cash stays in the market. Most changes in market value come from price, not from money moving between asset classes. This page separates the two for the funds it tracks.

Real flow for one day = shares created or redeemed that day × that day's value per share (NAV). Price effect = shares held × change in NAV. Fund companies report shares outstanding one day late, so the newest day has no flow figure yet. Dividend payouts show up as a small negative price effect. Longer periods are more reliable than single days.

Check: this method gives a SPY outflow of $6.98B for the week ending Nov 6, 2026. A published figure from TD Securities for that week was $7.0B.
